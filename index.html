How do steps become screen time?

Choose your walking rule in FeedFare. The native app defaults to 100 steps earning 5 minutes. For example, 500 steps earns 25 minutes at that setting. Use earned minutes for timed access to a selected app. Features and limits in older installed versions may differ.

Which permissions does FeedFare need?

Apple Health access lets FeedFare read step counts. Motion & Fitness provides live step counting. Screen Time access lets you choose apps to protect. Reminders are optional. See the setup guide for what each permission does.

Is the native app available now?

Native FeedFare is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Check the listing for your device’s compatibility and regional availability. These screenshots show the native app with example data, and appearance varies with your system settings.

Is FeedFare a one-time purchase?

The native release has a one-time US $4.99 download price, with regional prices shown on the App Store. Check the App Store for your regional price before downloading. The full native app is included without an in-app paywall, trial, or required subscription. Existing FeedFare owners receive full native access when they install this update on a supported device. Previous subscription plans have been retired and no longer renew. You can review previous charges and any remaining subscription period in Apple’s subscription settings.

Can my family share the purchase?

FeedFare supports Apple Family Sharing for eligible family groups. Apple manages purchase sharing and eligibility. Each person sets up FeedFare on their own device; their app selections and earned minutes remain separate. This does not provide remote parental controls or a shared minute balance. Learn about Apple Family Sharing.

Does it work with Apple Watch steps?

FeedFare can read steps that a compatible Apple Watch has synced to Apple Health on your iPhone. Those updates can arrive later. Open FeedFare and refresh if the total has not caught up. You do not need a watch to count steps with your iPhone.

What happens when a session ends?

In the native app, a session gives access to one selected app for the time you choose. Other selected apps stay protected, and the selected app is protected again when the session ends. Protection depends on Screen Time authorization. Get help with app protection.

Which devices are supported?

Native FeedFare supports iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Its appearance adapts to the system, including Liquid Glass on supported versions. Older devices may continue to run an earlier installed version with different features; check the App Store for compatibility and availability for your device.
