FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is FeedFare really free? +
Yes! Step tracking, goals, and streaks are 100% free forever. PRO is optional and unlocks cosmetics, themes, and advanced stats. You never need to pay to use the core app.
How does step tracking work? +
FeedFare uses Apple HealthKit and your iPhone's built-in motion sensors to count steps automatically. No extra hardware or wearable required — just bring your phone.
What happens if I miss a day? +
Your current streak resets to zero, but your all-time best streak is saved. It's a fresh start — and extra motivation to keep going tomorrow.
Can I cancel PRO anytime? +
Absolutely. PRO starts with a 3-day free trial and you can cancel anytime through your Apple ID subscriptions. No questions asked.
