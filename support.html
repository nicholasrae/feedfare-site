A little help, when you need it.

Start with the guide for your installed version. The new native design is in TestFlight; the current App Store release may use different button names.

Connect the features you want

  • Apple Health: in FeedFare Settings, choose Connect or refresh Health and allow access to step counts. You can review access from Apple Health’s app permissions.
  • Motion & Fitness: use Enable live steps. If permission was denied, review FeedFare under iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness, then return and retry.
  • Screen Time: choose Connect Screen Time and complete Apple’s authorization prompt. On the native preview, an existing connection shows Manage protected apps instead.
  • Reminders: choose whether to enable the evening check-in. You can change notification permission in iPhone Settings.

System restrictions can prevent permission changes. If an option is unavailable on a managed device, check with the person who manages it. Do not share your Apple Account password with support.

My steps are not updating

  1. Check that Apple Health itself has recent step data and that FeedFare has access to it.
  2. Open FeedFare and use Refresh steps or Connect or refresh Health. Apple Watch data may need time to sync to your iPhone.
  3. For live iPhone counting, enable Motion & Fitness and keep your phone with you as you walk. In the native preview, Refresh live steps shows progress and a result, a waiting state, or guidance.
  4. If a connection is already authorized, iOS may not show another permission prompt. Check the status and latest response instead.

Live Motion updates are a foreground feature. Background Health updates can arrive later; earned totals may not change immediately while FeedFare is closed. Opening the app refreshes available data. The website’s walking example does not read or track your actual steps.

My apps are not protected

  1. Check that FeedFare has Screen Time authorization. Open Apps or Settings and connect if needed.
  2. In the native preview, use Manage protected apps or Choose protected apps. Pick individual apps, or choose a category to add its current apps. Newly installed apps need to be selected later.
  3. Save the selection. The native preview supports up to 50 selected apps and does not protect websites. It explains mixed selections before saving.
  4. End an active session before changing the protected selection. A native session unlocks one chosen app while other selected apps remain protected.

If protection does not return after a session ends, open FeedFare to reconcile the session, check authorization, and contact support with the app version and what happened. Screen Time permissions and system behavior can affect enforcement.

Goals, streaks, and earned minutes

In the native preview, the default earning rule is 100 steps for 5 minutes. Change it in Settings. Changes apply to future credits; they do not rewrite the existing balance. Unused balance carries over. Streaks use a separate 1,000-step daily milestone, so changing your daily goal does not change the streak threshold. Features and limits in the public subscription version may differ.

Purchases and existing subscriptions

The App Store is the source for current regional prices and offers. The public app currently has optional in-app purchases. The native full-app paid-download release is being tested; this website does not change your billing.

  • Manage or cancel a subscription with Apple. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Request a refund from Apple. Eligibility depends on Apple’s policies and applicable law.
  • If access is missing in a subscription version, use its Restore Purchases control with the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  • The native preview does not require an in-app PRO purchase. If you previously subscribed, review that subscription separately. Installing an update or TestFlight build does not cancel renewal.

Resetting local data

In the native preview, Settings → Reset local data removes saved progress, balance, preferences, migration backups, app protection, and scheduled reminders on that device. This cannot be undone within the app. It does not delete Apple Health data, manage device backups, or cancel a subscription. Review what you need to keep before confirming a reset.

Still need a hand?

Email nick@nickrae.net with your FeedFare version/build, iOS version, what you tapped, and what you expected to happen. The native preview shows its build in Settings → About FeedFare. Please leave out private Health records, passwords, payment details, and Screen Time tokens. A screenshot with personal information removed can help.
